QUESTIONS

Good to know

Does it cost the customer anything?

FestivalQuote is free for customers during the launch validation period. You pay the provider directly if you choose to book.

Are the providers guaranteed?

No. Provider availability and pricing must be verified for your specific date and requirement before a quote is presented as available.

How many options will I receive?

We aim to return up to three relevant options when enough suitable providers are available.

What cities are supported?

Guwahati is the initial launch city. You can still submit a request for another city and we'll use it to measure demand for expansion.
