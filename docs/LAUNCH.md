# FestivalQuote — first 48 hours

## First objective

Prove that strangers will submit a real festival-service request and that local providers will answer.

## Demand

Publish one clear offer: "Tell us the service, city, date and budget. We’ll source up to 3 local options."

Use relevant local WhatsApp/Facebook/Instagram communities and direct responses to existing service-seeking posts. Avoid spam.

Track:
- requests
- city
- service
- event date
- budget
- source
- whether the requester asked for an introduction

## Supply

For each request, build a remote shortlist of 5–8 providers. Ask each for current availability and a quote. Keep provider details in the admin database.

## Closing the loop

Send 3 options with price, package, availability and provider contact. Record what the customer actually does next.

## Validation gate

Do not expand categories or buy ads until you have at least:
- 10 genuine customer requests
- 3 providers who respond
- 1 customer who asks for an introduction or takes a provider contact

Once the gate is met, double down on the category with the strongest evidence.
