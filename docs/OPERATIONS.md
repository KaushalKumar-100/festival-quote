# FestivalQuote — operating playbook

## Revenue-first workflow

1. Acquire demand from relevant local festival communities and people already seeking services. Do not buy ads before the first 10 qualified requests.
2. Qualify every request with city, service, date, budget and phone/WhatsApp.
3. Source 5–8 candidate providers remotely using public business pages, Google Maps and social profiles.
4. Ask candidates for availability, pricing and inclusions.
5. Return up to 3 comparable options to the customer by WhatsApp/email.
6. Keep the customer payment direct to the provider in the MVP. FestivalQuote does not hold customer funds.
7. After provider-side value is proven, test a qualified-lead fee such as ₹100–₹300. This is a test range, not a guaranteed market price.

## Initial launch

Start with one city and only 2–3 service categories. A practical test cluster is:
- Diwali decoration / lighting
- Pandit / puja service
- Photography / small event services

## Data discipline

Collect only information needed to source quotes. Store the provider source URL and the terms of the quote. Never put the admin key in frontend code.

## Unit economics target

At ₹150 average revenue per qualified lead:
- 10 paid leads → ₹1,500
- 50 paid leads → ₹7,500
- 100 paid leads → ₹15,000

These are operating targets, not guarantees.

## Spending rule

Keep the initial spend at ₹1,000 or less. Prefer ₹0 spend while validating demand. Spend only after real requests and provider responses are visible.
